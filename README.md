# Whiteface Mountain Cloud Water Chemistry Dataset (2018–2024)

This repository contains cloud water chemistry measurements collected at the summit of Whiteface Mountain Observatory in the Adirondack Mountains, New York, USA. 
The dataset includes measurements of major inorganic ions and low molecular weight organic acids in cloud water. 

 The file is sorted by sample ID, not dump date/time.
---

## Data Units

All analyte concentrations are reported in:

ppb (µg L⁻¹) or µeq L⁻¹unless otherwise noted. TOC_um is actually dissolved organic carbon (DOC) in uM C/L

---


## Organic Acid Measurements

The dataset includes measurements of low molecular weight organic acids (LMWOAs) in cloud water samples collected at Whiteface Mountain Observatory. Measurements were conducted across multiple years using ion chromatography at different laboratories depending on the sampling year.

### Detection Limits

Method detection limits (MDLs) are reported in mg L⁻¹. Detection limits vary by laboratory and analytical method used during different measurement periods.

| Organic Acid | Measurement Years | Laboratory | MDLs (mg L⁻¹) |
|---------------|------------------|-------------|---------------|
| Formate | 2018–2019, 2020–2021, 2022–2024 | AWI, Wadsworth Center, ASRC | 0.05, 0.0041, 0.0212 |
| Acetate | 2018–2019, 2020–2021, 2022–2024 | AWI, Wadsworth Center, ASRC | 0.05, 0.0119, 0.0285 |
| Oxalate | 2018–2019, 2020–2021, 2022–2024 | AWI, Wadsworth Center, ASRC | 0.05, 0.0265, 0.0237 |
| Pyruvate | 2018, 2020–2022 | AWI, Wadsworth Center | 0.05, 0.0167 |
| Glyoxylate | 2018, 2020–2022 | AWI, Wadsworth Center | 0.05, 0.0073 |
| Lactate | 2018–2019, 2020–2022 | AWI, Wadsworth Center | 0.05, 0.0041 |
| Malonate | 2018 | AWI | 0.05 |
| Succinate + Malate | 2018–2019 | AWI | 0.05 |

More details can be found here: https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/26/3951/2026/acp-26-3951-2026-supplement.pdf

#### Data Use Policy
These Whiteface Mountain (WFM) cloud water chemical monitoring data are made freely available to the public and the scientific community, and are the product of extensive research and effort to maintain and improve their accuracy and quality. However, the availability of these data does not constitute publication, and ASRC and their collaborating partners require citable outputs from their research to ensure its continued support. We therefore expect all users of this data to ensure that the responsible scientists receive fair credit for their work, and are offered the opportunity to contribute to the research uses of the data as they deem appropriate.

Accordingly, we ask that all users note and adhere to the following guidelines and procedures when using these data:
These data are freely available for exploratory analysis. At such time as a journal publication or conference presentation is planned using these data, the relevant Principal Investigators (PIs) of measurements included in the analysis should be informed of the nature and aims of the research project and the intended use of the data.
Contacting the PIs will ensure they have adequate foreknowledge of the proposed research and thus the opportunity to contribute as appropriate to the use of Whiteface Mountain cloud chemistry data in the proposed research project.
If the PI makes a significant intellectual contribution to the research, or if the data are essential to the work, or if an important result or conclusion depends on the data, co−authorship would normally be expected. This should be discussed at an early stage in the work.
Manuscripts using the data with ASRC co−authors should be sent to the coauthors for review well before (e.g. 4 weeks) they are submitted for publication, to ensure that the quality and limitations of the data are accurately represented and the coauthors have adequate time to provide input.
